# Code of Conduct

MTGeophysics.jl follows the [Julia Community Standards](https://julialang.org/community/standards/).
They apply to everyone taking part in the project: in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
other project space.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, Pankaj K Mishra, at
pankajkmishra01@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially.
